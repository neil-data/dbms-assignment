RELATIONAL ENTITY RELATIONSHIP

Institutional Data Integrity

Built on relational normalization standards, CEMS enforces foreign key constraints across Departments, Venues, Students, Events, and Registrations.

SELECT e.title, v.venue_name, COUNT(r.registration_id) AS enrolled,
       (e.max_capacity - COUNT(r.registration_id)) AS seats_available
FROM EVENT e
JOIN VENUE v ON e.venue_id = v.venue_id
LEFT JOIN REGISTRATION r ON e.event_id = r.event_id AND r.status = 'Confirmed'
GROUP BY e.event_id
ORDER BY enrolled DESC;
